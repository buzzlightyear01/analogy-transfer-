\section{Evaluation Design}
\label{sec:method}
We study three questions: \textbf{RQ1}, how do analogy conditions compare with alternative assistance and what answer changes accompany them; \textbf{RQ2}, does a source answer help more under intended correspondence than random assignment; and \textbf{RQ3}, what do explicit artifacts and corrected continuations reveal about unsuccessful staged paths?

\subsection{Tasks and accounting}
GPQA Main provides 448 graduate-level questions \citep{rein2023gpqa}: 78 biology, 183 chemistry, and 187 physics items in our local version. We use Main rather than Diamond. MuSR provides murder mysteries, object-placement questions, and team-allocation problems requiring multistep reasoning over narratives \citep{sprague2024musr}.

Accuracy uses the benchmark choice key. Recorded responses without a valid final answer count as incorrect. Missing records are distinct from recorded invalid answers. Matched comparisons align question identifiers. Repeated executions are kept with their question rather than counted as independent examples. Different tasks, samples, prompts, and providers preclude interpreting cross-study contrasts as controlled effects.

\subsection{RQ1: outcome attribution and transitions}
DeepSeek V4 Flash supplies concept-focused teacher analogies and non-analogical explanations for GPQA. Solvers are Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3, and Gemma-4-31B-it. Conditions include 300- and 600-word analogies; two 300-word or three 200-word examples; 300- and 600-word explanations; random same-domain and other-domain examples; CoT; and solver-generated analogy \citep{wei2022cot,kojima2022zero}. Word budgets are nominal, not compute matching. Appendix~\ref{app:execution} defines all conditions.

On shared items, let $H_{b\rightarrow c}$ be the fraction changing from incorrect under baseline $b$ to correct under condition $c$, and $D_{b\rightarrow c}$ the reverse. Then
\begin{equation}
p_c-p_b=H_{b\rightarrow c}-D_{b\rightarrow c}.
\label{eq:help-harm}
\end{equation}
Both fractions use all shared items as denominator. We also screen explicit references to the analogy, example, or teacher, including rejection. This lexical observation is separate from semantic transfer validation.

Llama, Qwen, and Mistral have 448 retained responses per condition. Gemma has 435--447, so its full-cohort accuracies can reflect composition differences. We do not interpret small unmatched Gemma differences as isolated treatment effects.

\subsection{RQ2: source type by answer availability}
GPT-5.6-Sol constructs a source with changed entities and context but intended shared solution relations for each selected MuSR target. It sees the target key; the solver does not. A source contains a narrative, question, choices, and source answer. Difficult examples receive manual review and a small model-based assessment; there is no exhaustive independent structural annotation.

The completed set comprises 103 questions: 61 murder mysteries, 21 object placements, and 21 team allocations, spanning 88 target stories. It is not a documented representative random sample of MuSR. Random controls permute the same source corpus one-to-one among targets with equal choice counts and no self-assignment. Same-story and same-task matches are reduced where feasible; four five-choice object-placement controls remain within a story.

The four cells are corresponding/answer withheld ($A$), corresponding/answer provided ($B$), random/withheld ($C$), and random/provided ($D$). Withholding removes only the answer field, retaining source choices. The target, neutral ``additional example'' label, instruction, temperature zero, and 2,048-token ceiling are fixed. We measure
\begin{equation}
\Delta=(p_B-p_A)-(p_D-p_C).
\label{eq:interaction}
\end{equation}
A positive interaction indicates a larger source-answer benefit under the designed correspondence. It does not validate every individual transfer, and can combine improvement in $B$ with deterioration in $D$.

Llama-3.2-1B and 3B and Llama-3.1-8B and 70B have three runs per cell; Qwen3-14B and 32B have two. Provider, version, and quantization differences prevent isolating parameter count. We average each question's executions before computing the contrast. Conditional 95\% resampling intervals use 20,000 target-story bootstrap samples, retaining all four cells and executions together (seed 20261012). They summarize target-story resampling with sources and assignment fixed; they do not quantify source-generation or random-permutation uncertainty.

Sensitivity analyses remove the four same-story controls and use the subset of questions with valid answers in every cell and execution. The latter conditions on an observed outcome and is a diagnostic check, not an unbiased replacement for full-cohort accuracy. We also compare matching and differing source/target answer positions; these strata contain different questions rather than randomized reorderings of the same target.

\subsection{RQ3: natural paths and assisted continuations}
\label{sec:phase3method}
The primary diagnostic cohort uses Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on 100 GPQA and 100 MuSR murder-mystery questions. Three sources are requested per target. Unsuccessful paths undergo the cumulative procedure in Section~\ref{sec:repair}. We report natural accuracy, initial assignments, continuation successes, and remaining deficiencies with explicit denominators.

Retained 100-item reports also permit question-matched direct/staged comparisons for Llama and DeepSeek V4 Flash on the same two cohorts. These provide performance context rather than a compute-matched superiority test. We also audit all 400 natural recorded paths for explicit input keys and for the schema content actually passed to adaptation, including paths with correct answers. Recorded downstream inputs are checked against their parent artifacts. This mechanical check does not validate relational adequacy. The full Llama stage trajectories are retained aggregate diagnostic records, not a new row-level reannotation. The four recorded stage templates share version \texttt{1.1-dev-6}; M includes suspect/motive examples even in GPQA, a possible domain bias. Some archived figures doubled counts for display; we undo that transformation and use the original 100-question cohorts.

Human review covers 30\% of the primary study's questions and associated outputs for coherence and correctness. The rest have no human review, and inter-annotator agreement is unavailable. We do not present stage assignments as validated ground truth. An additional saved DeepSeek schema-replacement case illustrates artifact inspection with a recoverable individual continuation; it is not a complete independently validated replication of all diagnostic trajectories.
